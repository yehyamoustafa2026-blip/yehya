HereYouGo — Digital Business Card Platform

  • Client

    Numbase LLC

  • Role

    Product Designer

  • Platform

    iOS & Android

  • Rating

    5.0 ★ (5K+ Users)

1. The Client

Numbase LLC created HereYouGo as an eco-friendly digital business card mobile platform available on iOS and Android . The app replaces wasteful paper business cards by empowering professionals to exchange contact details instantly through personal QR codes, direct links, dynamic card web pages, and a location-aware proximity networking engine .

2. My Role

Product Designer (End-to-End Execution) — I owned the complete product design lifecycle: user research, UX flow mapping, dark-mode visual UI execution, Figma component system creation, proximity networking UI architecture, interactive prototyping, usability testing, and front-end engineering handoff.

3. Problem Statement

Traditional paper business cards fail modern professionals during live events and daily networking :

  • • High Waste & Loss: Up to 88% of printed business cards are thrown away or lost within a week.
  • • Static & Unchangeable: When job titles, phone numbers, or websites change, entire physical card stacks become obsolete.
  • • Zero Engagement ROI: Professionals have no visibility into whether recipients ever viewed, saved, or interacted with their contact details after an initial meet-up.

4. Research & Discovery

Through user interviews with event attendees and sales executives, three core insights emerged: sharing contact details must take under 3 seconds without app-download friction for recipients, users want real-time analytics to measure networking effectiveness, and conference goers need a fast way to discover "People Nearby" in crowded venues without manually typing names .

5. Information Architecture

I established a clear 5-tab bottom navigation hierarchy that anchors all primary user workflows around speed and accessibility :

Home (QR Share & Quick Stats)  ➔  My Card (Editor & Preview)  ➔  Network (People Nearby)  ➔  Insights (Analytics)  ➔  Profile (Settings)

6. Design System Foundations

Engineered for high visibility in low-light networking environments and bright outdoors, the visual system uses an ultra-dark background palette (#0A0A0A and #1E1E1E) paired with a high-visibility neon electric green (#12CC00) for primary action buttons, online status dots, and conversion metrics . Typography is powered by Space Grotesk for bold structural headers and Inter for dense, high-legibility contact metadata .

7. Design Solution

The final application features a high-impact dashboard with single-tap QR sharing ("Scan to save my card"), a real-time proximity list ("People Nearby" displaying real-time distance metrics like 12m away), a self-service digital card customization suite, and a conversion analytics tab ("23 new profile views (+12% this week)") .

8. Usability Testing

Usability testing with 10 event attendees revealed that users needed immediate confirmation when another person successfully saved their card. I introduced quick visual feedback tiles (Visitors: 23, Saves: 46, Views: 128) directly on the Home dashboard, giving users instant gratification during live networking .

9. Implementation & Handoff

Drawing on my Computer Science background, I structured Figma components to match native mobile layout primitives and defined clean state logic for dynamic QR code rendering, location-proximity API updates, and Freemium tier feature locks ("Upgrade to Premium to reveal full identities").

10. Results & Impact

  • 5,000+ Downloads with a Perfect 5.0 ★ App Store Rating
  • Instant QR & Proximity Sharing eliminating physical paper card waste
  • Integrated ROI tracking with 1-tap native phone contact saving
  • Clear Freemium monetization funnel converting free cardholders to Premium analytics subscribers

11. Conclusion & Key Takeaways

HereYouGo proves how combining location-aware proximity UX with an accessible dark-mode UI system and value-driven personal analytics transforms traditional networking into a seamless, eco-friendly digital product .
