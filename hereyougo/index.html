HereYouGo — Digital Business Card Platform

  • Client

    Numbase LLC

  • Role

    Product Designer

  • Platform

    iOS & Android

  • Rating

    5.0 ★ (5K+ Users)

Project Overview

HereYouGo is an eco-friendly digital business card mobile app designed to replace traditional paper cards. It empowers professionals to exchange, save, and manage contact information instantly using personal QR codes, direct links, and a location-aware "People Nearby" networking engine.

My goal was to design an effortless, high-converting digital card system that minimizes friction at live networking events. The experience allows users to register in a single step, share their digital identity in seconds, and track profile engagement via integrated analytics.

  • 5,000+ Downloads with a Perfect 5.0 ★ App Rating
  • Instant QR Code Sharing & "People Nearby" Proximity Engine
  • High-Contrast Dark Theme UI System Built on Inter Typography
  • Custom Web Card URLs & Integrated Email Signature Generator

Design System & Color Architecture

The visual system was crafted specifically for high visibility under variable real-world lighting conditions (e.g., dim networking venues or bright daylight). Built upon an ultra-dark background palette (#0A0A0A and #3F3F3F), the UI uses a striking electric green (#12CC00) for critical interactive states, CTA buttons, and status indicators. Clean hierarchy is maintained across all components using Inter (Regular, SemiBold, Bold).

Key Impact & Results

By streamlining onboarding into a single step and implementing one-tap QR saving, the app eliminated traditional card exchange friction. Users can save contacts directly to their phone's native address book or maintain updated dynamic contacts within the app.

The addition of real-time card analytics ("23 new profile views") gave users tangible ROI on their networking efforts, while the eco-friendly digital model provided a modern, sustainable alternative that eliminates paper card waste.
