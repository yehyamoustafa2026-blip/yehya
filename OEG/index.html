OEG — Smart Tutor Finder Mobile App

  • Client

    Our Easy Game LLC

  • Role

    Product Designer

  • Platform

    iOS & Android

  • Rating

    4.9 ★ (10K+ Users)

Project Overview

OEG (Our Easy Game) is an on-demand educational technology platform that connects university and high school students directly with certified tutors for real-time homework assistance, exam preparation, and thesis support.

The challenge was to design an intuitive, frictionless mobile experience that balances two distinct user flows: students looking for immediate 24/7 AI or human tutor assistance, and tutors needing a clear system to review requests, submit bids, and manage schedules.

  • 10,000+ Active Student & Tutor Downloads
  • 4.9 ★ Rating on Google Play Store
  • 24/7 AI-Powered Study Assistant Integration
  • End-to-End Real-Time Request & Booking Stepper

Design System & Color Architecture

To maintain scalability across dozens of screens, I established a comprehensive Figma component design system. The visual identity utilizes a deep brand purple (#d040c9) for core navigation surfaces paired with vibrant orange action elements (#FF9800) for high-contrast CTA buttons. All color ramps were constructed with systematic 5% to 90% opacity scales to support clear UI hierarchy and accessible text contrast.

Key Impact & Results

By replacing fragmented WhatsApp tutor groups with a centralized, multi-step booking stepper (Request Created → Tutor Selection → Pending Payment → Completed), student drop-off rates were reduced dramatically.

The introduction of the 24/7 AI Assistant allowed students to receive instantaneous answers for late-night study sessions while seamlessly escalating complex queries directly to verified human tutors.
