Base360ai — AI-Powered B2C Communication Platform

  • Client

    Base360 Inc.

  • Role

    Product Designer

  • Platform

    Web & SaaS Landing Page

  • System

    AI Automation Engine

Project Overview

Base360ai is an all-in-one AI-powered customer engagement platform designed to unify every B2C communication channel—social comments (Meta, TikTok), DMs, WhatsApp, SMS, voice calls, and email—into a single intelligent workspace.

The challenge was to design a high-converting landing page and brand narrative that solves the fragmentation problem B2C brands face. The design illustrates how a single social comment (e.g., "how much is this?") triggers autonomous AI agents to respond, slide into DMs, qualify the lead into a built-in CRM, place AI voice calls, and enroll the user into ongoing email marketing.

  • 360° Unified Inbox for Meta, TikTok, WhatsApp, SMS, Calls & Email
  • Autonomous AI Agents for Comments, Lead Qualification & Voice Calling
  • Built-in Sales CRM with Automated High-Intent Lead Pipeline Tracking
  • Integrated Subscriber Capture, Email Newsletters & Campaign Analytics

Design System & Visual Architecture

To reflect the advanced AI capabilities of Base360ai, I established a dark-mode visual hierarchy with vibrant aura spotlight glows (electric blue, deep violet, and neon cyan accents). A modular four-card container grid was designed to articulate the four core platform capabilities clearly: Unified Inbox, AI Agents, Sales CRM, and Subscriber Marketing.

Key Impact & Results

By visually telling the story of a single social comment converting end-to-end into a closed lead, the landing page effectively bridges the gap between chaotic customer inquiries and tangible business revenue.

The structured four-pillar breakdown allowed enterprise B2C decision-makers to immediately grasp how AI agents handle repetitive support work while seamlessly escalating high-intent buyers directly to human sales teams.
