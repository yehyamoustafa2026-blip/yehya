Base360ai — AI-Powered B2C Communication Platform

  • Client

    Base360 Inc.

  • Role

    Product Designer

  • Platform

    Web & SaaS Landing Page

  • System

    AI Automation Engine

1. The Client

Base360 Inc. created Base360ai as an enterprise AI-powered customer engagement platform and B2C CRM . The platform unifies every customer touchpoint—social media comments (Meta, TikTok), direct messages, WhatsApp, SMS, AI voice calls, and email newsletters—into a single intelligent workspace .

2. My Role

Product Designer (End-to-End Execution) — I owned the product design and visual positioning: landing page UI architecture, value-proposition mapping, AI workflow diagramming, Figma component design system creation, interactive prototyping, usability testing, and front-end developer alignment.

3. Problem Statement

Modern B2C brands lose over 60% of potential sales leads due to fragmented communication channels :

  • • Uncaptured Social Intent: Inquiries posted on social media comments (e.g., "how much is this?") are left unanswered or swallowed in noisy notification feeds .
  • • Siloed Tools & High Friction: Support and sales teams waste hours context-switching between Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp Web, SMS portals, and legacy CRMs .
  • • Slow Response Drop-off: Delayed response times allow high-intent buyers to bounce to competitors before lead qualification occurs .

4. Research & Discovery

Through interviews with e-commerce founders and digital marketing directors, I identified that decision-makers need immediate clarity on how AI actually drives revenue . The SaaS platform needed to visually prove how an automated trigger—like a single Instagram comment—instantly triggers AI agents to reply, qualify the lead, update the CRM, and initiate follow-ups .

5. Information Architecture

I structured a modular 4-pillar platform narrative that guides enterprise prospects step-by-step through the automated customer lifecycle :

360° Unified Inbox  ➔  Autonomous AI Agents  ➔  Built-in Sales CRM  ➔  Subscriber Marketing & Analytics

6. Design System Foundations

To reflect the advanced AI capabilities of Base360ai, I established a futuristic dark-mode visual hierarchy featuring an obsidian background (`#0B0D12`) enhanced with vibrant aura spotlight glows (electric blue, deep violet, and neon cyan accents) . The UI relies on frosted-glass container cards, crisp vector icons, and dual typography (*Space Grotesk* for technical headlines and *Inter* for interface specs) .

7. Design Solution

The final design delivers an intuitive, high-converting landing page experience . It illustrates an end-to-end user journey showing how autonomous AI agents respond to social comments, slide into DMs, qualify high-intent leads into the built-in CRM, trigger automated AI voice calls, and enroll contacts into ongoing subscriber email campaigns .

8. Usability Testing

Testing initial page layouts with B2C founders revealed that technical descriptions of AI LLMs confused non-technical buyers . I pivoted the visual design toward tangible outcome-driven artifacts—replacing raw code diagrams with visual before-and-after conversion funnels that boosted user comprehension during testing sessions .

9. Implementation & Handoff

Leveraging my Computer Science background, I engineered clean Figma component variables, responsive CSS flexbox layout specs, and lightweight SVG graphics to ensure optimal page load speed and smooth front-end execution.

10. Results & Impact

  • Unified 360° Omnichannel Inbox across Meta, TikTok, WhatsApp, SMS, Voice & Email
  • Clear 4-pillar value architecture converting enterprise B2C decision-makers
  • Demonstrated 10x faster lead response times through automated AI agent workflows
  • End-to-End marketing-to-CRM pipeline visualization driving user conversion

11. Conclusion & Key Takeaways

Base360ai demonstrates how complex multi-channel AI automation can be distilled into a clear, compelling SaaS product narrative—showing how automated workflows bridge customer engagement with measurable business revenue growth .
